About Bankroll

About

Bankroll is an onchain financial cooperative built around one reserve asset, VLT — live on Ethereum since 2020, governed by immutable contracts rather than a company.

Overview Team Contact Brand kit

Overview

A cooperative, not a company.

Bankroll launched in 2020 and pioneered Proof of Liquidity: the idea that a digital asset should stand on verified onchain liquidity that no central party can pull. Everything since has been built on that premise.

01

Live since 2020

VLT has traded continuously on Ethereum for six years. The contract, its supply, and its liquidity have been publicly inspectable that entire time.

02

Proof of Liquidity

The market base matters more than the pitch. VLT's liquidity is secured onchain and verifiable by anyone before they participate.

03

Owned by nobody

Fixed 1.8M supply, no mint function, no admin keys, no pause switch. The rules cannot be rewritten after you join — not by a team, not by us.

04

Three pillars

VLT as store of value, VLT as collateral, and vltUSDC — the income layer that puts idle stablecoins to work in VLT's own market.

Team

Contributors, not custodians.

Bankroll is built by a small group of long-time contributors. That is deliberate, and it costs you nothing: the contracts are ownerless, so no contributor — past, present, or future — can move your funds, change the rules, or switch anything off.
